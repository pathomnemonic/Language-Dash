Delete your Language Dash account and data

Language Dash · Last updated 23 November 2026

Language Dash keeps its data in a database it shares with other apps from the same developer, and you sign in with a login that those apps may also use. So there are two separate actions in the app. You choose which one you want.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Language Dash and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  2. Open the Account tab (you must be signed in).
  3. Pick one of the two actions below and confirm. Both are deleted immediately and cannot be undone.

1. Delete my Language Dash account

Account → Delete my Language Dash account. This deletes your Language Dash account and everything Language Dash holds about it: your display name and picture, scores, friend requests and friendships, group memberships and any groups left empty, the activity items you posted and the kudos you gave or received, any study-buddy listing, your cloud save, and your Pro and Locker item records. A subscription that is still billing is cancelled first. Your sign-in is kept, because other apps may use it; you stay signed in and can create a new Language Dash account later if you wish.

2. Remove my sign-in (all apps)

Account → Remove my sign-in (all apps). This removes the login itself (your email or Google/Apple/Microsoft sign-in). That login is used by every app that shares it, not only Language Dash, so each app’s data for this login may be deleted as a result. It also deletes your Language Dash account and its data, cancels any subscription still billing and, if you signed in with Apple, revokes that Apple sign-in. You are asked to type REMOVE MY SIGN-IN to confirm.

What is not affected

Progress stored only on your device (your saved game, settings and custom words). Clear it through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, if you chose to send them, are not linked to you and cannot be traced back to delete (you can delete the usage statistics for a device in Settings → Data). Payment records kept by Stripe, Google or Apple stay with them as the law requires.

Cannot open the app?

Email patho.mnemonic1@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, say whether you want action 1 (delete my Language Dash account) or action 2 (remove my sign-in for all apps), and we will do it.
