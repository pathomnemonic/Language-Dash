## Remaining doubts

- ابنعم / ابنأخ / ابنةأخ (cousin, nephew, niece): joined two-word compounds with no single-word alternative; kept
- والد (parent): same as father-ish; suggest keep
- عشق (love): means passionate love; أحب is used for like; suggest keep
- عطلة (weekend): same as holiday family; the phrase is عطلة نهاية الأسبوع
- بالفعل (already), مازال (still), لاشيء (nothing): prefix/negation compounds, all standard but multi-part
- غرفة (bedroom): same as room; real term is غرفة نوم
- دان (owe): literary; كان مدينا is the common form
