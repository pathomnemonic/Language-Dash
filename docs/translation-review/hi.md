## Remaining doubts

- इस्तेमाल (use), काम (work), बात (talk), मदद, कोशिश, महसूस, पसंद, याद, प्यार, इंतज़ार, तय, उम्मीद: noun/verb stems that need करना; kept as the usual single-word form
- ढोना (carry): means lug; suggest उठाना if wanted
- खींचना (draw): draw a picture; चित्र बनाना is a phrase, kept
- बुलाना (call): telephone sense is फ़ोन करना; kept
- कभीकभी, कबका, कुछनहीं, कोईभी, एकबार: joined forms of two words; kept
- अभी (still): is really 'now/just'; अब भी is two words
- संबंध (belong), समाना (contain), आलिंगन (hug), नेतृत्व (lead), मार्गदर्शन (guide): bookish or loose sense, no better single word
- समुद्रतट (beach), हवाईजहाज़ (airplane): joined; two words in normal spelling
- कल (yesterday and tomorrow): same word in Hindi, unavoidable
- लाँघना (cross), रवाना (depart), भेंट (visit): loose sense; consider पार / निकलना / जाना
