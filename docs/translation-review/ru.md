## Remaining doubts

- 876 during: посреди means 'in the middle of'; correct is the phrase 'во время' / 'в течение' (no single word)
- 646 spell: no single Russian verb ('писать по буквам'); писать is an approximation
- 273 birthday and 720 welcome: hyphenated phrases are non-standard spellings but the only one-token options
- 260 afternoon: днем is an adverbial inflected form; 'после обеда' is the usual phrase
- 623 imagine: представлять (себе) is reflexive-ish; воображать is an alternative
- 446 thirsty: жаждущий is a participle; colloquial is 'хотеть пить' (phrase)
- 585 single: единственный means 'sole/only'; одиночный closer to 'single (individual)'
- 148 car: автомобиль is correct; машина is more everyday (also 502 machine = машина)
- 195 true: истинный is formal; правдивый / верный alternatives
- 216 away: прочь is somewhat literary
- 166 foot: стопа is anatomical; ступня is more everyday
- 18 because: поскольку is formal; 'потому что' is a phrase
- Spelling note: the table writes ё as е throughout (ребенок, зеленый) except всё; kept as is
